How the mission simulation works

  • Spread patternEach pass throws seed up to one lane width to each side, thickest in the middle. Neighbouring passes overlap so the total comes out even.
  • Wind driftSeed takes about 0.7 s to fall from 3 m, so wind carries it sideways. With compensation on, the drone shifts upwind to cancel the drift.
  • Variable rateA soil prescription map splits the paddy into three zones. The gate opens wider over weak soil and narrower over strong soil.
  • Battery and refillsThe battery drains faster with a full hopper. The drone returns when it runs out of seed or when it has just enough charge to get back, and the ground crew refills and swaps batteries at the pad.

Assumptions: 100 × 60 m paddy (0.6 ha), 25 kg hopper, 0.6 kg/s maximum gate flow, about 8 minutes of flight with a full hopper and 13 minutes empty, 10 s refill and 8 s battery swap, 3 m working height. Manual comparison assumes one worker hand-broadcasting about 0.3 ha per hour. These are estimates for the class MOC, not measured data.
